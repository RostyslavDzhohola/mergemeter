# EVAL: TabPFN vs logistic regression

Generated by `python scripts/eval.py` from the snapshots in `data/snapshots/` (fetched 2026-10-26). Re-run it to reproduce every number below.

**TabPFN beats the baseline on 7 of 8 repos; mean 5-fold ROC-AUC 0.853 vs 0.824.** On the harder time split (train on old PRs, test on new) it wins 3, ties 1 and loses 4 of 8: repos change their habits over time, and neither model sees that coming.

## Setup

- Data: the 300 most recent closed PRs per repo (GitHub GraphQL). Label = merged (1) vs closed without merging (0).
- Features: the 18 open-time features in `mergemeter/features.py` (size, files, tests/docs, maintainer/bot, author history, linked issue, title/body, topic labels). No comments, reviews or time-to-close.
- Author history is rebuilt inside every split from the training PRs only, so no held-out outcome reaches a training row.
- TabPFN: open-weight TabPFN v2 classifier checkpoint, local CPU, `n_estimators=4`, no tuning, no training loop (in-context learning).
- Baseline: median impute, log1p, standardise, `LogisticRegression(class_weight='balanced')`.
- **5-fold CV**: stratified, shuffled, seed 0, both models on identical folds (mean ± std).
- **Time split**: test on the newest 20 % of PRs by open date; train only on PRs that had *closed* before the oldest test PR opened (so fewer than 240 training rows).

## Caveats

- 300 PRs is a small sample; fold-to-fold spread (± std) is a few AUC points, so small gaps are noise.
- GitHub serves a PR's *current* size, files, title, body and labels, so edits after opening (or topic labels added late) can still carry some post-open information. Author history and verdict labels are handled; these are not.
- Repos where maintainers and bots drive nearly every merge (fastapi) are easy for both models: "who opened it" carries most of the signal.
- Where an AUC sits near 0.5 (gradio, and transformers on the time split), open-time features carry little signal for that repo. The app shows this per-repo AUC on its stat cards, so you can see when not to trust a score.
- TabPFN 2.5+ checkpoints are licence-gated and non-commercial; this eval uses the open v2 checkpoint that the app ships with.
