# EVAL: TabPFN vs logistic regression

Generated by `python scripts/eval.py` from the snapshots in `data/snapshots/` (fetched 2026-10-26). Re-run it to reproduce every number below.

**TabPFN beats the baseline on 7 of 8 repos; mean 5-fold ROC-AUC 0.895 vs 0.871.** On the harder time split (train on old PRs, test on new) it wins 3, ties 1 and loses 4 of 8: repos change their habits over time, and neither model sees that coming.

## Setup

- Data: the 300 most recent closed PRs per repo (GitHub GraphQL). Label = merged (1) vs closed without merging (0).
- Features: the 20 leakage-safe features in `mergemeter/features.py` (size, files, tests/docs, author history counted from *earlier* PRs only, first-24 h comments and reviews, linked issue, title/body, topic labels).
- TabPFN: open-weight TabPFN v2 classifier checkpoint, local CPU, `n_estimators=4`, no tuning, no training loop (in-context learning).
- Baseline: median impute, log1p, standardise, `LogisticRegression(class_weight='balanced')`.
- **5-fold CV**: stratified, shuffled, seed 0, both models on identical folds (mean ± std).
- **Time split**: train on the oldest 80 % of PRs, test on the newest 20 %: the "predict the future" check.

## Caveats

- 300 PRs is a small sample; fold-to-fold spread (± std) is a few AUC points, so small gaps are noise.
- `additions`, `deletions` and `changed_files` are the PR's final size, which can drift from its size at open time. Every other feature is known at open time or within 24 h.
- Repos where maintainers and bots drive nearly every merge (fastapi) are easy for both models: "who opened it" carries most of the signal.
- TabPFN 2.5+ checkpoints are licence-gated and non-commercial; this eval uses the open v2 checkpoint that the app ships with.
